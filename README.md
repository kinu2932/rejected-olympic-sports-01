## The line

> Competitive unsubscribing: athletes race to leave a mailing list — first place wins three more emails confirming it.

116 characters. `main.py` writes exactly this to `/output/entry.txt`
(or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` outside the grader), deterministically,
standard library only.

## Why this line

The field is saturated with queueing, napping, apologising, flat-pack
assembly and ironing — this entry takes a fresh angle no other entry uses:
email unsubscribing. Three punchlines stack on one pattern, the mechanism
defeating itself:

1. **Race to leave** — the sport is quitting a mailing list, an inverted race.
2. **The prize is more of it** — first place wins three *more* emails.
3. **The confirmation is the punishment** — the emails exist to confirm you left.

It sounds like a real event a broadcaster would have to explain with a
straight face, the twist lands before the line ends, and every word earns
its place.
